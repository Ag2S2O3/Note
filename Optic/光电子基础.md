# Requirements
## For group
One student from each group will be called **randomly** as the representative to make the presentation for the group. The presentation will last 15 minutes plus 5 minutes for questions and answers. **The 8 students in one group will be scored together for the seminar presentation.**
## For report
The paper should be no less than 1000 words but no more than 1500 words. The paper should contain a title, abstract, main text, references, and possibly figures and tables if necessary. Each paper will be scored individually.**The report should be prepared according to the manuscript requirement of Chinese Optics Letters.**
## Key points
You may address the following points in your report and in your presentation regardless
of which topics you have chosen:
1. Why are people interested in this type of device or system? What are the potential applications?
2. What are the key elements in the device structures or the system?
3. What are the working principles and device physics or the system?
4. What are the significant characteristics of this type of device or system? What are the state-of-the-art numbers for these characteristics? Are there any tradeoffs?
5. What are the materials used to make these devices? How are these devices made?
6. What are the major challenges that people need to address? Your own insight and comments on these challenges.
